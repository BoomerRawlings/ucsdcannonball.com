Submission criteria

Record your run

  1. Pick your line.

    Use any route and any means necessary to get from point A to point B as quickly as possible.

  2. Start at the arch. Finish at the bollards.

    Your time starts as you pass under the arch at the North Point Visitor Information Center. It ends as you pass between the bollards at Gilman Drive and Pepper Canyon Drive.

  3. Record the whole run.

    Start Strava or another GPS tracker before the arch. Stop recording after the bollards. Count every second between the two crossings, including stops.

  4. Send in your time.

    Enter your handle, elapsed time, and the date and time you finished in San Diego. If you used motor power at any point, pick Motorized. Otherwise, pick Human-powered.

Submission privacy

Who sees my route?

Send a GPS link the reviewer can open. Your route and optional notes stay private here. The board shows your handle, division, elapsed time, straight-line average speed, and when you finished.

Keep the link working until your time is verified. Then you can delete the GPS recording or turn off link sharing.

Where it started

The original Cannonball

In November 1971, the Cannonball field set off from a Manhattan parking garage for the Portofino Inn in Redondo Beach. Brock Yates and Dan Gurney made it in 35 hours and 54 minutes.

Ours starts under an arch and ends between a row of bollards. Part of the fun is comparing times with someone who took a completely different way across campus. Know a friend who could beat the record? Send them the time.
